# Slice 5: failure inventory

Real scripted tau, coding tools and git:

- A three-item plan briefs either dependent too soon, prepares it from the wrong head, serializes two available workers, loses a sibling's merge, or hands over a different tree from the verified full set.
- A seam finding loses its source revision, evidence or affected labels; an investigator cannot reproduce it; classification stops an unrelated worker, or an affected worker can publish before the owner answers. The unaffected item must verify and merge while triage is open.
- A kill after `build` commits but before the designer's revision round loses or repeats the decision or proposal. After reopening the same SQLite store, only the changed node needs a decision. The answer must not start a second piece of work.
- Approval rewrites merged work, ignores an edited or dropped unstarted item, or resumes a held worker before its new dependency merges or without telling it the changed contract. Landing and hand-over must cover the revised tree.

Cheaper lane contracts (separate lane tests, not repeated as process scenarios):

- A repeated filing forks the finding; a semantic duplicate fails to link to the original.
- A blocking filing holds a sibling before classification; dropping a finding fails to release its hold or releases another cause's hold.
- Two findings with one cause and decision get two answers; a batch whose members need different answers is admitted.
- A defect against the Mandate stops for an owner decision instead of going to the coordinator as discovered work.
- A revision rewrites an already-verified item instead of scheduling a correction as new work.
- A stale answer reopens or acts on a finding resolved another way.
- An edge case without likelihood is accepted; citing a blessed pattern opens another finding.
