# Mandate round failures

- Ground must have neutral, distinctly labelled observations before a precedent-setting request can advance. Follow still binds under prompt authority, never under a self-declared review.
- A draft cannot omit a Ground ruling, a falsifiable probe, a timed proof, callers, a fenced sketch, an alternative, a rider origin or an unambiguous label. A Ground ruling cannot be delegated to a reference.
- The review basis is an annotatable Mandate, including the proposed Ground rulings, caller-first seam sketches, actual proof commands and timeouts. Determined nodes are context, not decisions. Riders have separate decisions.
- Approval with any rider outstanding cannot start Build. Accept binds only that rider; leave_open does not bind it. A duplicate or withdrawn answer does nothing.
- An accepted incidental rider is a binding choice, not an invented domain rule that alone forces an Encode item.
- Rework returns numbered ranged annotations to the designer; missing, duplicate, extra or empty replies cannot publish the next revision. Both owner and designer can see the replies against their original text.
- A revision supersedes old decisions, preserves earlier revisions and dispositions, and does not inherit authority for changed content, a removed and reintroduced node, or a rider merely relabelled as binding text.
- A rider returned for design needs a real replacement under the same label. An already approved unchanged node keeps its approval; the changed and redesigned nodes return to review. Approval notes from earlier approving answers survive.
- Decline marks every annotation not pursued, withdraws all remaining decisions and leaves no active turn, effect or build.
- A stopped draft goes through the existing owner resume/cancel path, not Ground or spontaneous retry.
- Integration: scripted round answers, a pulled and cited principle, Encode, verifier project descriptions and a real landing. Killing the server between a round answer and the next revision preserves the annotation debt and the revision history.

Build-plan and principle delivery failures are inventoried beside their focused tests.
