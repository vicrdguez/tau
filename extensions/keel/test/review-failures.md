# Mandate round failures

- Ground without a neutral, distinctly labelled audit opens a draft turn.
- Incomplete Ground rulings, seams, probes, proof, alternatives or rider dispositions can enter review, or collide under duplicate labels.
- A review omits its goal, the fact that all nodes bind on approval (even nodes with references), or meaningful Ground, Mandate and rider content; it opens extra rider decisions.
- Approve fails to bind the shown riders as proposed, drops the approval annotation or starts Build more than once.
- A bound Mandate is not readable Markdown, misstates Ground rulings as proposed or presents tradeoffs and alternatives as binding.
- A bound incidental rider manufactures an Encode obligation when there are no seams or rules.
- Rework loses numbered ranges, excerpts or the stored revision; accepts incomplete or invalid replies; or shows the owner-facing instructions to the designer.
- A new or revised referenced node, new rider or changed rider disposition goes unmarked, or the review loses removed content or lists a removal as current authority.
- Decline leaves active work, silently loses still-owed notes or treats previously answered rework notes as outstanding.
- A stopped draft resumes as Ground, drops annotation debt or reuses its draft token.

Build-plan and principle delivery failures are inventoried beside their focused tests.
