# Build and Verify simplification: failure inventory

- A prose fence starts preparation; equivalent path spellings differ between worker and verifier fences.
- A changed principle or playbook causes a hold instead of normal publication, or its Git-reported path is lost before the item verifier sees it.
- The item verifier is not told that either kind of policy file should be unchanged unless the item was tasked with changing it.
- The verifier sees serialized Mandate data instead of the governing text and a rendered proof command.
- The first plan's numbered replies are lost between Build handoff and the owner hand-over, or not paired with the original approval notes.
- The hand-over title identifies a commit but not the goal of the work.

The real-repository reviewed-work scenario retains the numbered revision replies, Encode, migration, merge and landing checks under one review per round. The SQLite recovery scenario retains its one owed revision and no-build checks after the rider decisions are removed.
