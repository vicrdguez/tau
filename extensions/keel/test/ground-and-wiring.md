# Slice 3 failure inventory: Ground and installed composition

- A precedent-following Mandate binds once under the prompt with no owner ask.
- Ground refuses missing authority, duplicate node labels, missing probes and invalid proof commands without adopting.
- Precedent-setting work is recorded with a plain owner notice and no build.
- An ambiguity requires two distinct readings. Only the current owner's answer leads to another Ground turn with the selected reading and annotations.
- Declining the ambiguity closes it without a build.
- A stopped Ground turn files one holding finding. Resume delivers its evidence and direction on a fresh token; cancel withdraws the turn.
- The version-1 Design document has an independent fixture, including its Mandate.
- The installed workflow registers its four kernel tasks and the real lane tools, and gives only Ground to the entry designer.
- Every active role has instructions for its own tools and only its own model override.
- Repository discovery uses configured branches, and effect execution uses its stored snapshot rather than discovering new ref targets on retry (real Git scenarios).

Build, Verify, Queue, landing serialization and both end-to-end scenarios have separate inventories and tests.
