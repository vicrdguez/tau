# Override and sweep failure inventory

## Override

- A blank or unannotated rejection fails to ask again or files a check anyway.
- An annotated rejection loses an annotation, the source commit, or its original hand-over evidence.
- An investigator closes an override, or triage build does not seed work that encodes it.
- Duplicate override wording makes two outstanding obligations, or defer/drop forgives the check.
- An unrelated, failed, cancelled or unchanged build clears a check without a verified landed commit.
- Real repository: the owner rejects, builds from triage, and sees no check owed on the encoding hand-over.

## Sweep and queue

- An unset interval runs a sweep; idle projects never run one; a tick stops after failure or repeats across restart.
- A sweep interferes with a landing; a project finding holds work or cannot be investigated and built independently.
- A sweep loses drift evidence, gives unreviewed or cancelled design authority, or cannot revisit a deferred batch once.
- A counterexample to a blessed pattern is discarded; filing alone revokes it.

## Principles

- An observed principle violation fails to return work or fails to file separate policy evidence.
- A binding or removal proposal loses the recorded evidence, repeats without new evidence, or updates policy without the owner's answer.
