# Queue-seeded Design and revisions: failure inventory

- A follow seed loses its text, reference or route, lets Ground substitute authority, or waits for needless approval.
- A precedent seed bypasses the neutral audit and the regular Mandate review, including after its designer stops and resumes.
- A proposal takes effect before the plan, reviews unaffected nodes, or omits the before/after evidence. Only Build's acknowledgement of the approved proposal binds the change.
- An unchanged, malformed or out-of-scope edit reaches review; an unclassified change must not invalidate an unaffected brief.
- A changed structured seam escapes the usage-first seam contract or loses the bound proof, rider or reference context.
- Rework loses numbered owner direction or reuses the answered decision. Approval notes do not reach the coordinator.
- Decline closes the work, updates its bound Mandate, or forgets the queue causes to release.
- A second triage answer is rejected, replaces an open round, or is drafted against the superseded Mandate. Serialize proposals durably through decline and apply.
- A stopped revision turn disappears, resumes as Ground, or reuses a delivery token.
- A late approval, acknowledgement, or proposal after cancellation or close reopens work.
- A process kill after triage loses the revision turn or repeats it (covered by scripted live-plan scenario).
